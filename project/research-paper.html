Super High-rise Building Innovation

An independent peer-reviewed review paper on technological innovation in super high-rise building design and construction, published in Highlights in Science, Engineering and Technology.

Read Article

Overview

Published an independent review paper synthesizing engineering literature on structural design, advanced materials, and construction technologies for super high-rise buildings.

Research Focus

  • Structural systems
  • High-performance materials
  • BIM construction management
  • Ultra-high concrete pumping
  • GPS and BDS surveying
  • Sustainable construction methods

Implementation Notes

  • Conducted a literature-based review on structural design, material selection, and construction technologies for super high-rise buildings in China.
  • Compared major structural systems including tube structures, steel structures, and frame-shear wall systems, summarizing their performance, limitations, and safety considerations.
  • Analyzed innovative materials and construction methods such as high-performance steel, high-performance concrete, BIM-based construction management, ultra-high pumping concrete, and GPS/BDS surveying technologies.
  • Synthesized findings from engineering literature into practical reference points for safer, more efficient, and more sustainable high-rise building design and construction.
